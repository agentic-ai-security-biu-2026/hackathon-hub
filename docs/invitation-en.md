Dear STEM students (B.Sc. and advanced degrees),

We are pleased to invite you to a focused hackathon on one of the hottest topics in security and AI: **securing enterprise agentic AI systems (Agentic AI Security)**.

The event is a collaboration between **Red Hat**, **Nebius**, the **Bar-Ilan University Faculty of Computer Science**, **UNIC** (venture-building studio), the **Bridge to Industry** program, and the **Bar-Ilan University Cyber Club**.

## Key dates

| Milestone | When / where |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Kickoff** | **Sunday, 13 December 2026**, Bar-Ilan University |
| Arrival & registration | 09:30–10:00 |
| Opening & intro talks | 10:00–13:00 |
| Team formation, challenge matching, start of work | 13:00–15:00 |
| **Build period** | ~1.5 weeks of team work (teams of **3–6**) |
| **Submission deadline** | **22 December 2026, 23:59** |
| **Closing & winners** | The following week at **Red Hat offices, Raanana** (selected teams + org/company reps) |

## Challenges and tracks (by difficulty)

Designed primarily for **B.Sc.** students, and also for **M.Sc. / Ph.D.** students who want a research angle:

| # | Challenge | Diff | Notes |
|---|-----------|------|-------|
| **T0** | **Office Manager CTF** | 1–2 | Optional Nebius-owned attack on-ramp |
| **Ch1** | **Credential Escape Room** | 3 | Blast-radius containment after credential compromise |
| **Ch2** | **Policy Compiler** | 3–4 | NL policy → enforceable controls (grad-friendly) |
| **Ch3** | **Seven Layers Deep** | 5 | Defense-in-depth hardening (hardest) |

Stub pages and starter repos live on this site and in the GitHub org.

## Why participate

- **Hands-on tech:** industry platforms (OpenShift AI, Nebius Token Factory, related agent stacks)
- **Mentoring:** cyber and industry experts from Red Hat and Nebius
- **Networking:** present work to industry and academia leaders
- **Career & entrepreneurship:** link to Bar-Ilan innovation / UNIC mentoring
- **Prizes & recognition:** cash prizes, certificates, Red Hat developer-blog / channel exposure
