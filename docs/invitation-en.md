Dear STEM students (B.Sc. and advanced degrees),

We are pleased to invite you to a focused hackathon on one of the hottest topics in security and AI: **securing enterprise agentic AI systems (Agentic AI Security)**.

The event is a collaboration between **Red Hat**, **Nebius**, **UNIC** (venture-building studio), the **Bridge to Industry** program, and the **Bar-Ilan University Cyber Club**.

## Challenges and tracks

The hackathon is designed primarily for **B.Sc.** students, and also for **M.Sc. / Ph.D.** students who want a research / academic angle. Example tracks at varying difficulty:

1. **Credential Escape Room** (medium–high) — agent privilege escape and defenses (e.g. SPIFFE/SPIRE). *(Maps to planned Ch1.)*
2. **Policy Compiler** (recommended for grad / research) — natural-language security policy → automated enforcement controls. *(Maps to planned Ch5.)*
3. **Seven Layers Deep** (advanced) — deep hardening of a multi-layer model-based agent. *(Maps to planned Ch7.)*
4. **Office Manager CTF** — experiential attack/defense challenge. *(Maps to optional Track 0; Nebius-supported.)*

Open-challenge freeze still follows registration volume (see `01-tracks-…` / Topics doc).

## Why participate

- **Hands-on tech:** industry platforms (OpenShift AI, Nebius Token Factory, related agent stacks, and more)
- **Mentoring:** cyber and industry experts from Red Hat and Nebius
- **Networking:** present work to industry and academia leaders
- **Career & entrepreneurship:** link to Bar-Ilan innovation / UNIC mentoring
- **Prizes & recognition:** cash prizes, certificates, Red Hat developer-blog / channel exposure (see prize Option 1 in planning docs)
